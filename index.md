I am interested in how international events come to be personally meaningful. 

Currently, I am engaged in projects looking into the gendered differences in responses to national security threats, the unique impact of epistemic uncertainty on international security, and the impact of international competition on public support for funding international organizations, among others. 

With a focus on psychological mechanisms, my research reveals the hidden structures of public opinion for issues where it is considered most haphazard while challenging existing assumptions that bracket the human mind.
